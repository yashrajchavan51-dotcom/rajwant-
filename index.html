Our Story

A house built on trust, since 1985

RAJWANT — The Palace of Jewels began as a single counter in Thane and has grown into one of the city's most recognised bespoke jewellery houses, now with showrooms at Hiranandani Meadows and near Talao Pali.

For four decades, the same principle has guided every piece leaving our workshop: authentic hallmarking, fair making charges, and craftsmanship that treats each order as an heirloom in the making — not a transaction.
